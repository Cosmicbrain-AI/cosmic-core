# CosmicBrain design system

## Direction

A welcoming robotics workshop: warm paper, thoughtful engineering, friendly robot sketches, and the people behind physical intelligence. The user requested a personal, warm, physics and robotics themed redesign with interactive robot elements. This direction supersedes the earlier cinematic American Dream brief for this redesign.

## Type and color

- Display: Instrument Serif, regular and italic. Headlines 41–82px; section headlines 41–56px.
- Body/UI: DM Sans, 400–700. Body 14–18px, comfortable 1.75–1.9 line height.
- Technical notes: DM Mono, 400/500. Use short 9–11px labels, never long body text.
- Paper/background #f7f4ec; card #fffdf7; ink #292b25; secondary ink #6c6d61.
- Terracotta/action #b94e32, white action text. Sage #5b7054 for scientific diagrams.
- Border #d9d7cb; stronger border #b6b7a7. Quiet sage #eeeee2 and clay #f0e8da section backgrounds.
- No forced dark mode. Operational status colors retain distinct meanings.

## Composition

1240px content, generous whitespace, asymmetric editorial layouts. 40–104px combined side margins; single columns below 760px. Fine rules and graph-paper diagrams create an engineering notebook. Cards reserved for meaningful collections and interactive workbenches. Buttons are lightly rounded; drawings can be irregular. Desktop header includes three plain-language wayfinding links; mobile navigation exposes all destinations.

## Interaction

Robot wave, blueprint toggle and arm slider; learning-loop tabs; native FAQ disclosure; existing catalog filtering; industry pilot checklist; contextual contact dialog. Inputs need visible labels, keyboard focus and clear selected states. Honor reduced motion. Never imply a concept illustration is a live robot connection, live telemetry, or deployed hardware. Scientific equations include plain-language meanings.

## Content

People first, concrete tasks, small steps. Preserve product scope: capture/refine/deploy, teleoperation, robotics data, robot catalog and industry discovery. Use existing contact endpoint and video. Avoid unverified deployment metrics, autonomy promises, invented team quotations, customer testimonials and guaranteed commercial outcomes. Keep backend authentication and robot controls intact.
